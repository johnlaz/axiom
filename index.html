From ticker to score in four steps

Every score is built the same way, so you can check it.

  1. 1

    Pull the numbers

    Prices, RSI and the 200-day average come from Yahoo Finance. Add a free FMP key and analyst targets and ratings join in.

  2. 2

    Read the headlines

    Recent stories come from Yahoo Finance, MarketWatch and CNBC feeds. Old stories are dropped, and every link opens the original article.

  3. 3

    Ask the AI to explain

    A Groq model gets only that data plus today's date. It is told not to invent prices, targets or news.

  4. 4

    Get a score you can check

    You see a 1–10 Key Action Score with a bull and a bear case. Prices, RSI and targets in the cards come straight from the data, not from the model.

When the data isn't there, AXIOM says so.

  • No analyst key? The analyst cards read "Unavailable" instead of showing a guess.
  • News feeds down? You see no headlines, never invented ones.
  • AI unreachable? You still get a score computed from the numbers alone, labeled as such.

Ask AXIOM

Chat with an analyst that knows your portfolio and risk profile. It is told today's date and given your numbers, so it has less room to guess.

Is GLCR stretched after this run?
Not yet. RSI is 58, so it is not overbought. It sits 11% above its 200-day average and 4% under its 52-week high. For a moderate-risk profile, a pullback toward the 50-day average would be a calmer entry.
What would change your view?
A close below the 200-day average, or RSI above 70 while analyst targets stay flat. Either would weaken the bull case.

Questions people ask

Something missing? Write to lazlab.io@gmail.com.

Does it cost anything?

AXIOM is free. Groq offers a free API key, and Financial Modeling Prep has a free tier of 250 calls a day. The FMP key is optional.

Do I need an API key to try it?

No. Without keys you can explore live quotes, the market scanner, portfolios and watchlists. A Groq key unlocks the AI features, including Analyze and Ask AXIOM. An FMP key adds analyst targets, ratings and confirmed earnings dates.

Where does the data come from?

Prices, charts and the major indices come from Yahoo Finance. Headlines come from Yahoo Finance, MarketWatch and CNBC feeds. Analyst targets and ratings come from Financial Modeling Prep. Free quotes can be delayed, so check your broker before you trade.

Is this financial advice?

No. AXIOM is for research and paper trading. Scores and AI commentary are not recommendations, and the AI can be wrong. Check the numbers and decide for yourself.

Does it work offline?

The app itself opens offline after your first visit. Prices, headlines and the AI need a connection.

How do I install it?

Open the app, then use your browser's install option: Add to Home Screen on iPhone and Android, or the install icon in the address bar on desktop.
